Space-at-Scale (web): privacy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

  • The simulation runs entirely in your browser. It does not collect, store or transmit personal data, and it sets no cookies.
  • The page is served by Cloudflare for maxey0.com. Cloudflare processes ordinary request data (such as IP address and request time) to deliver the page; the Maxey0 site's request logs are covered by the Maxey0 Context Plane privacy policy.
  • Optional Claude panel. If you choose to enter your own Anthropic API key, the page keeps it in memory only (never saved, never sent to maxey0.com) and sends your questions, the simulation values Claude asks for, and the key directly from your browser to api.anthropic.com. That use is governed by Anthropic's terms and privacy policy and billed to your key. Reloading or closing the page forgets the key.
  • Links to papers and catalogs open only when you click them.

The Space-at-Scale Claude plugin is a separate product with its own policy: plugin privacy policy.
